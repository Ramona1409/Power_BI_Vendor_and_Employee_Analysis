# Vendor & Employee Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing vendor operations and employee-related performance indicators. The report explores trip activity, revenue, trip distance, geographic distribution, daily and hourly patterns, average income, and tips across vehicle types.

The dashboard contains two main sections: Vendor Analysis and Employee Analysis.

## Business Objectives

Analyze total trip volume and revenue.

Explore trip distribution across boroughs and days of the week.

Identify the busiest pickup hours.

Examine average income by day and hour.

Compare average tips per mile across vehicle types.

Provide data-driven insights to support operational planning and performance analysis.

## Tools & Technologies

**Power BI Desktop** – dashboard development and data visualization.

**Power Query** – data preparation and transformation.

**CSV datasets** – source data for the analysis.

## Dashboard Overview

## 1. Vendor Analysis

This dashboard explores trip activity, revenue, average distance, geographic distribution, and pickup-time patterns.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Trips: 789K

Total Revenue: 13.56M

Average Distance: 2.82 km

### Key Findings

Manhattan records the highest trip volume among the displayed boroughs.

The busiest hours among those displayed are 1 PM to 5 PM, with 3 PM showing the highest trip volume.

Tuesday and Monday show the highest trip volumes among the displayed days.

Trip activity varies across boroughs, days of the week, and vehicle types.

### Business Recommendations

Consider prioritizing vehicle availability in Manhattan, where trip volume is highest.

Use hourly trip patterns to support driver scheduling and vehicle allocation.

Review daily demand patterns to identify opportunities to improve operational planning.

Monitor trip volume and revenue together to understand changes in business activity.

## 2. Employee Analysis

This dashboard focuses on average income, average tips, average tips per mile, and variations by day, hour, and vehicle type.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Average Income: $17.19

Average Tip: $1.69

Average Tip per Mile: $0.60

### Key Findings

Monday has the highest average income among the displayed days.

The 5 PM hour has the highest average income among the five displayed hours.

Medium Car shows a slightly higher average tip per mile than Private vehicles.

Average income and tip metrics vary across the displayed days, hours, and vehicle types.

### Business Recommendations

Investigate the patterns associated with higher average income on Mondays and at 5 PM.

Compare average tips per mile across vehicle types to understand differences in observed performance.

Monitor income and tip indicators over time to identify meaningful changes.

Use additional data to investigate whether the observed patterns are consistent across different periods and operating conditions.

## Key Takeaways

The dashboards provide an overview of trip activity, revenue, geographic distribution, and employee-related performance indicators. The visualizations help identify differences by borough, day, hour, and vehicle type, providing a starting point for further operational analysis.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Power BI to organize operational data into interactive dashboards, track key performance indicators, explore patterns, and communicate potential business insights.
